TRANSPARENCY & CONFIGURATION

Settings & Methodology

Understand how this demonstration works and how to interpret its outputs.

Risk classification

Configurable score bands

High risk is at or above the high threshold. Medium risk is at or above the medium threshold but below high. Other customers are low risk.

System capabilities

What the current version does and does not do

Implemented in this demo

Browser-based CSV import, customer filtering, transparent heuristic scoring, visualizations, and rule-based retention recommendations.

Not a trained predictive model

Risk scores are not calibrated probabilities. Default metrics and behavioral relationships are illustrative, not evidence from a real subscription business.

For production use

Train and validate a model on historical data, evaluate calibration and performance, protect customer data, and test retention interventions before deployment.
